        Hi. I’m Rip.

        Made with love by a fellow millennial.

        Remember when music was something you had? A burned CD with the track list in Sharpie. A Discman that skipped if you breathed on it. Sitting through dial-up for one song that turned out to be a stranger’s voice saying “gotcha.” A mixtape that ate a whole Saturday and meant everything. And twelve CDs for a penny, as long as you didn’t read the fine print.

        Then came the algorithms. The apps. The subscriptions. Now nearly everything is rented, nothing is really yours, and the shuffle knows you better than you’d like.

        uPod can’t give those days back. Nobody can. But it’s a step in the right direction: your music, on your own hardware, in your own house. No ads, no monthly fee, nobody deciding what you hear next.

        Simple music. Music you own.

        P.S. Please don’t leave me on the dashboard in July.
